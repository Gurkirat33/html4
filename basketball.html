History of Basketball

Basketball was invented by Dr. James Naismith in December 1891 in Springfield, Massachusetts. He created the game as a less injury-prone sport than football for young athletes to play indoors during the cold winter months. The game quickly gained popularity and spread globally through the YMCA movement.

Basic Rules and Scoring

Court and Equipment:

  • Rectangular court (94 feet × 50 feet in NBA)
  • Basket height: 10 feet
  • Basketball (29.5 inches circumference for men)

Scoring System:

Shot Type Points Description
Free Throw 1 point Uncontested shot after a foul
Field Goal 2 points Shot from inside the three-point line
Three-Pointer 3 points Shot from beyond the three-point line
