History of Football

Football, or soccer, has ancient origins dating back over 2,000 years. Modern football began in England in the 19th century, with the formation of the Football Association in 1863. Today, it's the world's most popular sport, played by millions and watched by billions.

Rules and Regulations

Basic Rules:

  1. Two teams of 11 players each
  2. Match duration: 90 minutes (two 45-minute halves)
  3. Goal scored when the ball crosses the goal line
  4. No handling the ball (except goalkeeper within penalty area)
  5. Offside rule applies

Common Fouls:

  • Pushing or holding an opponent
  • Handling the ball
  • Dangerous tackles
  • Obstruction
