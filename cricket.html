History of Cricket

Cricket, often known as the gentleman's game, originated in south-eastern England during the 16th century. By the end of the 18th century, it had become the national sport of England. The expansion of the British Empire led to cricket being played overseas, and by the mid-19th century, the first international matches were being held.

Basic Rules and Equipment

Essential Equipment:

  • Cricket bat (made of wood)
  • Cricket ball (leather with cork core)
  • Stumps and bails
  • Protective gear (helmet, pads, gloves)

Basic Rules:

  1. Two teams of 11 players each
  2. One team bats while the other bowls and fields
  3. Runs are scored by:
    • Running between wickets
    • Hitting boundaries (4 runs or 6 runs)
  4. Ways to dismiss a batsman:
    • Bowled
    • Caught
    • LBW (Leg Before Wicket)
    • Run out
    • Stumped
