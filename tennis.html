History of Tennis

Tennis evolved from a 12th-century French game called "paume" (palm). The modern game of tennis originated in Birmingham, England, in the late 19th century. The first Wimbledon Championship was held in 1877, marking the beginning of professional tennis tournaments.

Basic Rules and Scoring

Scoring System:

  • Points: 0 (Love), 15, 30, 40, Game
  • Games needed to win a set: 6 (with 2-game lead)
  • Sets needed to win (men's): Best of 5
  • Sets needed to win (women's): Best of 3

Basic Rules:

  1. Server must:
    • Stand behind baseline
    • Hit ball diagonally across court
    • Clear the net
  2. Ball can only bounce once before return
  3. Players alternate serving games
  4. Change ends after odd-numbered games in a set
